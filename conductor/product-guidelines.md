# Product Guidelines - KBMS

## Tone & Voice
- **Professional & Reassuring:** Use clear, direct, and authoritative language that emphasizes reliability and safety. Given the critical nature of battery systems, the prose should instill confidence and provide definitive information.

## Visual Standards
- **Standardized Data Visualization:** Maintain strict consistency in color coding for status indicators:
    - **Green:** Normal/Healthy operation.
    - **Yellow/Amber:** Warning/Attention required (Non-critical).
    - **Red:** Critical/Action required (Danger).
- **Utility-First Branding:** Keep brand elements (logos, secondary brand colors) minimal and non-distracting. The user's focus should remain entirely on the data and system status.

## UI/UX Patterns
- **Subtle Alert Integration:** Warnings should be integrated into existing UI components (e.g., changing the color of a card or adding an icon) to maintain a seamless user experience while still being discoverable.
- **Material Design Principles:** Adhere to Material Design for consistent spacing, shadow usage, and component behavior.
