# Product Definition - KBMS (Battery Monitoring System)

## Target Audience
- **Individual Owners of Off-Grid Power Systems:** Specifically users with RVs, boats, or solar setups who need reliable, portable monitoring of their energy storage.

## Core Goals
- **System Reliability:** Provide users with the confidence that their off-grid power system is operational, helping them avoid unexpected blackouts.
- **Battery Longevity:** Enable users to maximize the lifespan of their expensive battery banks through proactive monitoring of health and temperature.
- **Energy Optimization:** Help users understand their discharge patterns and charging efficiency to optimize their energy consumption.

## Key Features
- **Real-Time Monitoring:** Live dashboard displaying critical metrics: State of Charge (SOC), Voltage, Temperature, and Current (In/Out).
- **Customizable Alerts:** User-defined thresholds for low voltage, high temperature, or critical SOC levels with push notifications.
- **Device Management:** Simple interface to discover, connect, and configure multiple battery units or BMS modules.

## Visual Identity & UX
- **Clean & Modern (Material Design):** The application will follow Material Design principles, utilizing bold typography, intuitive iconography, and purposeful whitespace to ensure a user-friendly and accessible experience.
