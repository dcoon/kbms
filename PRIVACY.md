Privacy Policy for KBMS
Last Updated: October 26, 2023

This Privacy Policy explains how KBMS ("the App") handles information. KBMS is a free, open-source mobile application designed to monitor and configure KiloVault HLX+ LiFePO4 batteries.

1. No Data Collection
We believe in your right to privacy. KBMS does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage statistics, or analytics.

Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other identifying information.

Sensitive Information: We do not access your contacts, photos, or location history.

2. Local Data Processing
The App interacts with your hardware locally via Bluetooth.

Battery Data: Data retrieved from your KiloVault batteries (such as voltage, temperature, and cycle counts) is processed in real-time on your device.

Storage: This data is not saved to a remote server. Any logging you enable within the "Settings" menu remains on your device unless you manually choose to export and share those logs for troubleshooting purposes.

3. Permissions
To function correctly, KBMS may request the following permissions:

Bluetooth: Required to scan for and connect to nearby KiloVault HLX+ batteries.

Local Logs (Optional): If you choose to "Share Logs" for troubleshooting, the App will use standard system sharing tools to allow you to send a text file to a destination of your choice.

4. No Third-Party Integration
The App does not include third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics trackers (such as Google Analytics or Facebook SDK). Your data is never sold to or shared with third parties.

5. Open Source Transparency
As an open-source project licensed under the MIT License, the source code for KBMS is available for public inspection. You can verify our data handling practices by reviewing the code at github.com/dcoon/kbms.

6. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and within the repository.

7. Contact Us
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub Issues page.
